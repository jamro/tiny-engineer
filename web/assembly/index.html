Build it step by step

Follows the assembly guide, one small step at a time: each step shows the robot so far and slides the new parts in.

    Stop. Center at 90° before attaching
    1. Plug the servo into any PCA9685 channel (channel mapping does not matter for centering).
    2. Power the ESP32. Join the setup Wi‑Fi (TinyEngineer-XXXX), open http://192.168.4.1/config, open Assembly support, and press Move all to 90°. Use Identify on a joint if you need to see which plug is which.
    3. After that joint, unplug the servo from the PCA9685 before the next awkward join.

    Do not run guided range calibration or finish the wizard (OLED, LED, speaker, home Wi‑Fi) until the final setup section. Centering in the assembly guide
