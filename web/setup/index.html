Setup

On-robot wizard

Use your phone. It is the easiest way to tap through setup while you stand next to the robot.

  1. 01

    Connect power

    The robot starts a Wi-Fi network named TinyEngineer-XXXX.

  2. 02

    Open setup on your phone

    Join that Wi-Fi, then open http://192.168.4.1/config.

  3. 03

    Walk through every screen

    1. Servos. Tap Calibrate servos and follow Body, Neck, Head, then Hands.

    2. Screen. If the text is upside down, tap Rotate 180°.

    3. RGB mapping. Tap Red, Green, and Blue. Change the color order only if a color looks wrong.

    4. Speaker. Tap Play and listen for the welcome clip.

    5. Connection. Set the robot’s name and your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz only), or choose USB connection only.

  4. 04

    Power off, then on

    When the wizard is finished, unplug power, then plug it back in.

  5. 05

    It joins your Wi-Fi

    The robot connects to your home network. Try a pose at http://tiny-engineer.local/.

Open control page http://tiny-engineer.local/
